What Types of Steel Beam Do We Supply?

We supply a full range of structural steel sections to suit any project in York and the surrounding area. Here is a breakdown of the most common beam types we work with:

Universal Beams (UB) and RSJ Beams

The most widely used beam type for residential and commercial projects. Universal beams, commonly known as RSJ (Rolled Steel Joist) beams, are used wherever a horizontal load-bearing span is required. Knocking through walls, creating open-plan spaces, supporting floors above openings and new build structural frameworks are all typical applications. We supply these cut to your exact length with holes, plates and any other fabrication requirements already done before delivery.

Universal Columns (UC)

Where a vertical load needs to be transferred down through a structure, a universal column is the answer. Commonly used as internal posts, padstone supports and as part of larger steel frames. We supply and install UC sections across York for both residential and commercial projects.

Steel Lintels

For smaller openings such as windows, doorways and garage openings, a steel lintel is often the most cost-effective and structurally sound solution. We supply lintels sized to your specification and can fit them as part of a wider installation package.

Plates, Brackets and Connections

A steel beam on its own is only half the job. End plates, web cleats, base plates and custom connection brackets are all part of what we fabricate and supply. Everything is drilled and prepared before it reaches site, keeping your build programme on track.

Frequently Asked Questions: Steel Beams in York

How much does a steel beam cost in York?

The cost depends on the size, grade and whether installation is included. A standard RSJ for a residential opening typically ranges from £150 to £600 for the steel alone. Installation adds to this depending on complexity. Contact us for a free, no-obligation quote specific to your project.

Do I need planning permission to install a steel beam in York?

In most cases, structural beam installations fall under Building Regulations rather than planning permission. You will typically need Building Control approval, and your structural engineer's beam specification will be part of that process. Always check with your local authority if you are unsure, particularly for listed buildings or properties in York's conservation areas.

Do you supply steel beams only, without installation?

Yes. We offer supply-only as well as supply and fit. If you have your own team for installation, we are happy to fabricate the beam to your specification, cut to length, with holes and plates as required, and deliver to your York site.

What size steel beam do I need for removing a wall in York?

The beam size depends on the span and the load it carries from above. A structural engineer should calculate this for any load-bearing wall removal. Ryan at Ultra Fabrication can also give guidance based on experience with similar residential projects across York and Yorkshire.

Do you offer crane delivery for steel beams in York?

Yes. For larger beams or difficult access sites, we can arrange crane delivery to any location across York and Yorkshire. This is arranged as part of the quote process so there are no surprises on the day.
