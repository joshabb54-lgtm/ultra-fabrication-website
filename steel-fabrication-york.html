Working on York's Historic and Listed Buildings

York has more listed buildings per square mile than almost any other city in England. The city's Victorian terraces, Georgian townhouses, medieval commercial buildings and 20th century developments all present different structural challenges when it comes to steel. We have worked on projects ranging from knocked-through walls in Edwardian semis in Fulford to structural beam replacements in listed commercial properties in the city centre.

When working on York's heritage properties, our approach is to fabricate exactly what the structural engineer specifies, finish it correctly, and install it with minimum disruption to the existing structure. Our bespoke fabrication capability means we are not limited to standard sections when the project calls for something different.
