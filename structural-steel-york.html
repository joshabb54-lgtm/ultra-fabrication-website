Structural Steel Services in York

Mezzanine Floors

A mezzanine floor is one of the most effective ways to add usable floor space to a commercial or industrial building in York without extending the footprint. We design and install steel mezzanine floors to your specification, including the structural frame, decking, edge protection and access stairs. All steelwork is fabricated at our Escrick workshop and installed on site with minimal disruption to your operation.

Platforms and Walkways

Industrial and commercial sites across York often require elevated working platforms and walkways for equipment access, storage or process flow. We fabricate and install steel platforms and walkways to meet your load requirements and site constraints, complete with handrailing and anti-slip decking where required.

Access Stairs

Steel access stairs are a core part of our structural work. We fabricate straight, dogleg and switchback stair configurations to suit your building layout, including stringers, treads, risers and handrails. All stairs are fabricated to the relevant standards and can be finished with paint, galvanizing or powder coating depending on the application.

Fencing and Gates

Steel fencing and gates for residential, commercial and agricultural properties across York and Yorkshire. We fabricate bespoke gate and fencing panels to your design and size, with full installation available. Whether you need a simple farm gate or a set of automated entrance gates for a commercial property, we can help.

Ladders

Fixed steel ladders for roof access, plant access and industrial applications. Fabricated to your height and specification with safety cage options where required.

Steel Frame Structures

For residential extensions, outbuildings, agricultural buildings and commercial structures across York, we fabricate and erect complete steel frames. Working from your architect or structural engineer's drawings, we produce the steelwork in our Escrick workshop and erect it on site. Steel beam supply and installation for infill panels and openings is included as part of this service where required.

Areas We Cover for Structural Steelwork

We carry out structural steel projects across a wide area from our Escrick workshop. Our key areas include York and the surrounding villages, Selby and the Vale of York, Harrogate and North Yorkshire, Pocklington and East Yorkshire, and Wetherby along the A1 corridor. For agricultural structural steelwork we also cover a wide rural area across North and East Yorkshire.
