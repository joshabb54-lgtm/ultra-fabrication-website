Who We Are

Steel fabricators specialising in structural steel supports, with over 10 years supplying customers across the North of England.

Ultra Fabrication is a leading provider of steel fabrication services. We specialise in manufacturing high-quality steel beams for various industries, from residential new builds to large commercial projects. Our team is dedicated to customer satisfaction and delivering cost-effective solutions that do not compromise on quality.

With a friendly and experienced staff, we ensure a fast turnaround time on all projects. Our priority is customer satisfaction and we strive to maintain a high quality of work, fast turnaround and remain cost efficient on every single job we take on.

Based at Bell Farm Stead, Escrick, York, we operate across Yorkshire and the wider North of England, offering both workshop fabrication and full onsite installation services.
