Steel Fabrication Harrogate

Premium steel fabrication for Harrogate and the surrounding area. Structural beams, bespoke metalwork and coded welding from our Yorkshire workshop.

Harrogate is one of Yorkshire's most affluent towns, with a high-value residential property market and a significant commercial sector. The demand for quality structural steel and bespoke metalwork in Harrogate is strong, driven by high-end residential extensions, luxury apartment conversions, commercial refurbishments and new build developments across the town and surrounding area.

Ultra Fabrication serve Harrogate from our workshop in Escrick, approximately 22 miles to the south east. We provide structural steel beam supply and installation, bespoke steel fabrication and coded welding services for residential and commercial projects across Harrogate, Knaresborough, Ripon and the surrounding villages.
