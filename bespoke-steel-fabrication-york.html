What We Mean by Bespoke Steel Fabrication

Bespoke fabrication covers any steel component or assembly that cannot be bought off the shelf. In practice that means:

  • Curved or cambered beams for architectural openings and feature structures
  • Non-standard section sizes or profiles fabricated from plate or built-up sections
  • Custom connection brackets, gusset plates and cleats to specific structural requirements
  • Architectural metalwork for interior and exterior applications
  • One-off assemblies for heritage and listed building projects in York
  • Prototype or single-item fabrications that are not economic for large fabricators to take on
  • Steel components to designer or architect drawings where standard products are not suitable

Curved Steel Beams in York

One of our most requested bespoke services is curved steel beam fabrication. Open-plan residential conversions, contemporary extensions and heritage restorations across York and Yorkshire often require beams that follow an arc rather than running in a straight line. This is a technically demanding piece of work that requires both the right equipment and the experience to get the curve accurate across the full length of the section.

Ultra Fabrication have completed multiple curved beam projects for new build and renovation projects across York, and one of our most cited Google reviews specifically references the quality of a curved beam fabrication carried out for a complex new build project. It is a capability we are genuinely proud of.

Working with Architects and Structural Engineers in York

We regularly work from architect and structural engineer drawings on bespoke projects. If your project has a design team in place, we are experienced at reading fabrication drawings, asking the right questions and translating what is on paper into a finished steel component. We can also advise at an early stage on the most economic way to fabricate a particular detail, which can save cost and programme time.

If you do not have drawings, we can work from sketches, descriptions or site measurements. Ryan is experienced at taking a client's concept and developing it into something that can be fabricated and installed safely and effectively.

Bespoke Metalwork for York's Heritage Buildings

York has one of the richest concentrations of listed buildings and conservation areas in England. Structural and architectural metalwork in these settings requires care, precision and an understanding of what Building Control and heritage officers will and will not accept.

We have experience working on projects within York's conservation areas and on listed buildings across North Yorkshire, fabricating steel solutions that are structurally sound, sympathetic to the existing fabric and acceptable to the relevant authorities. If your project is in a sensitive location, call us early in the design process so we can advise on what is achievable.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can you fabricate curved steel beams in York?

Yes. Curved beam fabrication is one of our specialisms. We have completed multiple curved beam projects for residential and commercial projects across York and Yorkshire.

Do you work from drawings or sketches?

Yes. We work from architect and structural engineer drawings, from hand sketches, or from a verbal description and site measurements. We are experienced at turning concepts into finished steel.

Can you do bespoke metalwork for listed buildings in York?

Yes. We have experience working on heritage and listed building projects across York and North Yorkshire, fabricating steel solutions that meet structural and conservation requirements.

What is the minimum order for bespoke fabrication?

There is no minimum order. We regularly take on single-item fabrications and one-off jobs. Call Ryan to discuss your project however small or large.

How long does bespoke fabrication take?

Timescales depend on the complexity of the job and the current workload. Straightforward bespoke items can often be turned around within a week. More complex fabrications may take longer. We give realistic timescales at the quoting stage.
