Our Metal Work Services in York

Cutting and Drilling

Precision cutting and drilling is at the foundation of quality metal work. We cut steel sections to exact lengths and drill holes to specified positions and diameters, ensuring that every component arrives on site ready to install without modification. This saves your site team time and keeps your build programme on track.

Painting and Priming

Exposed steelwork needs to be protected against corrosion, particularly in Yorkshire where weather conditions can be demanding. We offer painting and priming as a standard part of our fabrication process. Whether you need a simple red oxide primer for steelwork that will be covered up, or a full paint system for exposed architectural steel, we can accommodate your specification.

Shot Blasting

Shot blasting removes mill scale, rust and contamination from steel surfaces to provide a clean key for paint systems. It is the preferred preparation method for structural and architectural steelwork that will be exposed to the elements or where paint adhesion and longevity are critical. We arrange shot blasting as part of our fabrication and finishing service.

Galvanizing

For steelwork that will be permanently exposed to the weather, such as gates, railings, external platforms and agricultural structures, hot-dip galvanizing provides the most durable corrosion protection available. We arrange galvanizing through our network of approved processors, coordinating collection, processing and return to keep your programme moving.

On-Site Installation Across York

Our installation team covers all areas of York and the wider Yorkshire region. We handle the full installation process including:

  • Delivery to site including crane delivery for larger components
  • On-site lifting assistance and beam placement
  • Site welding of connections and plates
  • Bolting and fixing of structural connections
  • Final alignment and levelling checks
  • Site painting and touch-up of any disturbed areas

We know that lifting steel beams on site is physically demanding and requires careful planning. Our team lends a hand throughout, making the installation process as straightforward as possible for your site team.
