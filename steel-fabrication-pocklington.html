Steel Fabrication Pocklington

Steel beams, welding and metal work for Pocklington and East Yorkshire. Approximately 16 miles from our workshop, serving the Wolds gateway area.

Pocklington sits at the foot of the Yorkshire Wolds, approximately 13 miles east of York. The town has a growing residential market with significant new build activity on its fringes, alongside a strong agricultural sector across the surrounding East Yorkshire landscape. Ultra Fabrication serve Pocklington and the surrounding area with structural steel, welding and metal work services for all project types.

We cover not just Pocklington itself but the wider East Yorkshire area including Market Weighton, Stamford Bridge, Barmby Moor and the agricultural communities across the Wolds. Agricultural steel work, farm building structures and rural residential projects form a significant part of our work in this area.
