Welding Services We Offer in York

MIG Welding

MIG welding (Metal Inert Gas) is one of the most versatile and widely used welding processes for structural and fabrication work. It produces clean, strong welds on mild steel, structural sections and fabricated assemblies. We use MIG welding for the majority of our workshop fabrication work and for onsite structural connections where conditions allow.

MMA Welding (Stick Welding)

MMA welding, also known as manual metal arc or stick welding, is the preferred process for outdoor and onsite work where wind, moisture or awkward access makes MIG less practical. It is particularly useful for site welding of steel beams and structural connections in York's often unpredictable weather conditions.

Why a Coded Welder Matters on Your York Project

The term coded welder refers to a welder who has passed a formal qualification test to a recognised standard, such as BS EN ISO 9606. This certification demonstrates that the welder can produce welds to a specified quality level consistently under test conditions.

On structural projects in York, particularly those involving steel beams and load-bearing connections, Building Control will often expect welding to be completed to a certified standard. Using a coded welder at Ultra Fabrication gives you and your structural engineer confidence that every weld meets the required specification.

Many smaller welding operations in and around York cannot offer this. We can.

Frequently Asked Questions: Welding in York

Do you offer mobile welding in York?

Yes. We travel to your site anywhere across York and Yorkshire and supply our own power. No mains electricity connection required.

Are your welders coded?

Yes. All structural welding is carried out by a fully coded welder meeting industry standards. This is important for Building Control approval on structural projects.

What is the difference between MIG and MMA welding?

MIG is typically used in workshop conditions and produces clean, fast welds. MMA (stick) welding is more versatile outdoors and in difficult access situations. We use whichever process is most appropriate for your project.

Can you weld steel beams onsite in York?

Yes. Site welding of structural steel beam connections is one of our most common services. We work with builders and contractors across York regularly.
